# VIDEO & AUDIO

## SCREENSHOTS

Take a screenshot with Flameshot
$ flameshot gui

Take a screenshot with GNOME Screenshot
$ gnome-screenshot

Take a screenshot with Spectacle
$ spectacle

Take a screenshot on X11
$ scrot

Save a screenshot with maim
$ maim [fileName].png

Take a screenshot on Wayland
$ grim [fileName].png

## SCREEN RECORDING

Record the screen on X11
$ ffmpeg -f x11grab -i :0.0 recording.mp4

Record the screen on Wayland
$ wf-recorder -f recording.mp4

Record a terminal session
$ asciinema rec session.cast

## WEBCAM

Take a photo from a webcam
$ fswebcam [fileName].jpg

Take a webcam photo with FFmpeg
$ ffmpeg -f v4l2 -i /dev/video0 -frames:v 1 [fileName].jpg

## PLAY AUDIO & VIDEO

Play a media file with mpv
$ mpv [file]

Play a media file with VLC
$ vlc [file]

Play a media file with FFmpeg
$ ffplay [file]

Play a WAV file
$ aplay [file]

## VOLUME

Open the ALSA mixer
$ alsamixer

Open the PulseAudio/PipeWire mixer
$ pavucontrol

Increase the default output volume
$ pactl set-sink-volume @DEFAULT_SINK@ +5%

Toggle mute on the default output
$ pactl set-sink-mute @DEFAULT_SINK@ toggle

Mute the default ALSA output
$ amixer set Master mute

Unmute the default ALSA output
$ amixer set Master unmute

## RECORD AUDIO

Record from the default microphone
$ arecord -f cd [fileName].wav

Record audio with FFmpeg
$ ffmpeg -f alsa -i default [fileName].wav

## SPEECH & BEEP

Make the computer speak
$ spd-say "I am a robot"

Use eSpeak for speech synthesis
$ espeak "I am a robot"

Play a system beep
$ beep

## INSPECT MEDIA FILES

Show media codecs, resolution, duration, and bitrate
$ ffprobe [file]

Show detailed media information
$ mediainfo [file]

## CONVERT MEDIA

Convert between video formats
$ ffmpeg -i input.avi output.mp4

Convert MP4 to WebM
$ ffmpeg -i input.mp4 output.webm

Extract audio as MP3
$ ffmpeg -i video.mp4 -vn -b:a 192k audio.mp3

Resize a video while keeping the aspect ratio
$ ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -vf scale=1280:-1 output.mp4

## CUT VIDEO

Cut a video without re-encoding
$ ffmpeg -ss 00:01:30 -i input.mp4 -t 00:00:20 -c copy clip.mp4

Cut and re-encode for more accurate cuts
$ ffmpeg -ss 00:01:30 -i input.mp4 -t 00:00:20 clip.mp4

## IMAGES & VIDEO

Create a video from numbered images
$ ffmpeg -framerate 24 -i image%d.jpg video.mp4

Extract frames from a video
$ ffmpeg -i video.mp4 image%d.jpg

Create an animated GIF
$ ffmpeg -i video.mp4 -vf "fps=10,scale=480:-1" animation.gif

Convert a GIF to WebP
$ gif2webp [inputFile] -o [outputFile]

Create an animated WebP
$ img2webp [inputFile] -o [outputFile]
